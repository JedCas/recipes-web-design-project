Roasted potatoes recipe

Ingredients:

  • Potatoes
  • Beef or Chickenstock
  • Butter
  • Oil
  • Herbs (Rosemary, Thyme)
  • Spices (Salt, Pepper, Paprika)

Instructions:

  1. Cut potatoes as desired

  2. Bring pot of salted water to a boil
    Then boil potatoes until soft

  3. Take out potatoes, strain and put in container.
    Shake container to rough up potatoes and leave to dry

  4. Mix chosen stock with oil.
    Preheat butter in air fryer at about 180 Celsius (Without tray)

  5. Add Potatoes into air fryer with spices, herbs and Stock-Oil mix.

  6. Fry until golden brown and crispy. Toss and turn after 10 mins.
